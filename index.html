Why AgeCal Pro

Built like an instrument, not a form

Most age calculators hand back a single number and stop there. AgeCal Pro treats your birth date as the start of a running measurement — a live dial that keeps ticking, a countdown that keeps closing in, and a set of life stats most calculators never bother to show.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is ever sent anywhere or stored — the dial resets the moment you close the tab.

Questions

Good to know

How does AgeCal Pro calculate my exact age?

It takes your date of birth and measures the exact distance to this moment, broken into whole years, months, days, hours, minutes and seconds — recalculated live, every second.

Can I find out what day of the week I was born on?

Yes — once you wind the clock, your birth day of the week appears in the info strip beneath the dial.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. Every calculation happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, logged, or saved.

What's the "Life in Numbers" section?

A set of rough, fun estimates — heartbeats, full moons witnessed, days spent asleep — built from your exact age. They're back-of-the-envelope averages, not medical or scientific measurements.
